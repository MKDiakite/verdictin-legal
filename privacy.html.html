VerdictIn Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

VerdictIn  ·  Effective: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VerdictIn handles information in connection with its content and TikTok integration.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

VerdictIn does not collect, store, or process personal information from viewers. We do not operate a website with user accounts, forms, or data submissions.

2. TikTok Integration

VerdictIn uses TikTok's Content Posting API solely to publish video content to the VerdictIn TikTok account. This integration accesses only the VerdictIn account credentials and does not access or store any viewer or third-party user data.

3. Third-Party Platforms

VerdictIn publishes content on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook. These platforms have their own privacy policies that govern how they collect and use data from their users.

4. Reddit Content

VerdictIn sources story content from publicly available Reddit posts. No private Reddit user data is accessed. Only public post titles and body text are used.

5. Cookies and Tracking

VerdictIn does not operate a website that sets cookies or uses tracking technologies.

6. Children's Privacy

VerdictIn content is intended for audiences 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact VerdictIn through our TikTok or Instagram social accounts.
